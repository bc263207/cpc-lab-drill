About

A practice tool for the IBSC CP-C exam. Arm A, this drill, is the lab layer. Arm B (disposition scenarios) is next.

  • Basic asks normal or abnormal. Values are clearly inside or clearly outside the reference range.
  • Advanced adds the third option, abnormal but this patient's baseline, and covers trend versus snapshot, medication effects on labs, outpatient anticoagulation, BNP interpretation and rule-out logic.
  • Fiendish uses near-normal values, look-alike distractors, specimen-integrity and unit traps, and values that look alarming but change nothing today.

Every value is generated inside a band from the lab table and never inside the safety margin around a cutoff, so a number is never one that one laboratory would flag and another would not. Every creatinine, hemoglobin, BNP and troponin comes with a stated baseline. Each session draws roughly 70% field-obtainable values and 30% record-review values.

Era: the literature cutoff is about 2016 to match the exam. Sepsis-3 lactate threshold, textbook troponin range, digoxin 0.8–2.0, warfarin, PT and aPTT are in; DOACs, SGLT2 inhibitors, high-sensitivity troponin and age-adjusted D-dimer are out.

No names, emails or scores are collected or stored.

Ungraded drill. Reference ranges from the course textbook; where a clinical range differs, the table row says so.
